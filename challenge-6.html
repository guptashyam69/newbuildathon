Background

Sports organizations, schools, colleges, and academies often manage registrations, tournaments, attendance, player performance, coaching, and facilities through disconnected or manual systems. Athletes also lack affordable tools for performance analysis, injury monitoring, and personalized training. There is a growing need for intelligent digital platforms that improve sports administration, athlete development, and the overall sporting experience.

Problem Statement

Design and develop an innovative Sports Technology solution that digitizes sports operations, improves athlete performance, enhances event management, and strengthens fan engagement. Participants are encouraged to integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Computer Vision, IoT, Wearable Devices, Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, AR/VR, or Blockchain where relevant.

Objectives

  • Digitize sports administration
  • Improve athlete performance using data
  • Simplify tournament and event management
  • Enhance coaching and player analytics
  • Increase fan engagement
  • Build scalable, secure, and user-friendly solutions

Modules

  • Sports Event & Tournament Management
  • Athlete Performance Analytics
  • AI Match Statistics Dashboard
  • Fitness & Training Planner
  • Injury Tracking & Recovery Monitoring
  • Team & Player Management
  • Smart Attendance using QR/NFC
  • Facility & Ground Booking System
  • Fan Engagement & Live Updates
  • AI Sports Assistant (Bonus)
  • Wearable Device Integration (Bonus)
  • AR/VR Training Experience (Bonus)

Innovation Opportunities

Computer Vision-based player tracking, AI coaching recommendations, wearable analytics, injury prediction, automated highlights, smart scoreboards, digital certificates, blockchain ticketing, multilingual support, accessibility features, and community sports platforms.

Expected Deliverables

  • Working Prototype (Web/Mobile/Desktop)
  • Source Code Repository
  • Project Presentation (PPT)
  • System Architecture Diagram
  • Database Design
  • UI/UX Design
  • Project Documentation
  • Live Demonstration

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any frontend, backend, database, cloud platform, AI/ML framework, computer vision library, IoT platform, or wearable SDK suitable for their solution.

Constraints

  • Complete the solution within the 4-hour hackathon duration
  • Use any programming language or framework
  • Open-source libraries and APIs are allowed
  • AI-assisted development is permitted if participants can explain their implementation
  • Solutions must be original and suitable for real-world deployment
