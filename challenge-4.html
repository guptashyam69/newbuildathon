Green Future

Building Smart Solutions for Environmental Sustainability and Climate Resilience

Rapid urbanization, pollution, deforestation, climate change, waste generation, water scarcity, and inefficient resource management are major environmental challenges. Governments, industries, and communities require innovative digital solutions.

🚀 Register for this Track ← Other Challenges

Background

Rapid urbanization, pollution, deforestation, climate change, waste generation, water scarcity, and inefficient resource management are major environmental challenges. Governments, industries, and communities require innovative digital solutions that enable monitoring, conservation, and sustainable decision-making using emerging technologies.

Problem Statement

Design and develop an innovative Environment & Sustainability platform that helps individuals, communities, industries, or governments monitor environmental conditions, reduce waste, conserve natural resources, and support climate action. Use AI, ML, IoT, GIS, Satellite Data, Cloud Computing, Computer Vision, Blockchain, and Data Analytics wherever relevant.

Challenge Objectives

  • Reduce environmental impact
  • Promote sustainable resource management
  • Improve waste segregation and recycling
  • Monitor air, water, and soil quality
  • Encourage climate awareness and community participation
  • Build scalable and affordable green technologies

Innovation Opportunities

AI-powered waste classification, computer vision recycling systems, IoT environmental sensors, drone-based monitoring, satellite analytics, carbon credit platforms, gamified sustainability, blockchain traceability, multilingual support, accessibility, and offline-first solutions.

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any frontend, backend, cloud platform, AI/ML framework, GIS tools, IoT devices, drones, or public environmental datasets.

Constraints

  • Build within the 4-hour hackathon duration
  • Open-source libraries and APIs are allowed
  • AI-assisted development is permitted if participants understand their implementation
  • Solutions should be practical, scalable, and environmentally sustainable
