About the Event

What is
Buildathon?

Buildathon is a high-intensity AI hackathon designed to challenge students and innovators to build real-world solutions across 9 technology domains. From Agriculture to Cybersecurity — your ideas matter.

  • 🤖

    AI-First Approach

    Leverage Machine Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, and Generative AI to solve real problems.

  • 🌍

    Real-World Impact

    9 carefully curated challenge tracks covering critical sectors of society and technology.

  • 🎓

    Open to All Students

    Teams of 2-4 students. All academic years, streams, and colleges welcome.

  • 🏅

    Exciting Rewards & Recognition

    Surprise rewards, certificates, mentorship opportunities, and industry recognition.
