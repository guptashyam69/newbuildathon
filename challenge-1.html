Track 01 Social Welfare Open Track

Connected Communities

Building Digital Solutions for Inclusive Social Welfare

Communities continue to face challenges accessing government schemes, social services, healthcare, education, employment opportunities, and NGO resources. Bridge these gaps with digital innovation.

🚀 Register for this Track ← Other Challenges

Background

Communities continue to face challenges in accessing government schemes, social services, healthcare, education, employment opportunities, volunteer support, disaster relief, and NGO resources. Information is often fragmented, processes are manual, and vulnerable populations struggle to receive timely assistance. Technology can bridge these gaps by connecting citizens, volunteers, NGOs, and public agencies through secure and accessible digital platforms.

Problem Statement

Design and develop an innovative Social Welfare platform that improves access to essential services, strengthens community participation, simplifies welfare delivery, and supports underserved populations. Participants are encouraged to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Cloud Computing, Internet of Things (IoT), GIS, Blockchain, and Data Analytics to create secure, scalable, and impactful solutions.

Challenge Objectives

  • Improve access to welfare services for underserved communities
  • Connect citizens with NGOs, volunteers, and government agencies
  • Increase transparency in service delivery and resource allocation
  • Enable faster assistance during emergencies and disaster situations
  • Promote inclusive and accessible digital services for all demographics
  • Build scalable solutions with measurable, demonstrable social impact

Innovation Opportunities

AI-powered welfare assistants, multilingual interfaces, offline-first mobile apps, smart matching between beneficiaries and volunteers, GIS-based service mapping, blockchain for transparent donations, predictive analytics for resource allocation, accessibility-first design, and digital identity integration.

Expected Deliverables

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any suitable frontend, backend, database, cloud platform, AI/ML framework, GIS tools, public APIs, or open-source technologies. There are no restrictions on technology stack choice.

Constraints

  • Build the solution within the 4-hour hackathon duration (4 hours)
  • Open-source libraries and public APIs are allowed
  • AI-assisted development is permitted if participants can explain their implementation
  • Solutions should be practical, secure, and scalable for real-world deployment
