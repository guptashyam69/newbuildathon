Background

Educational institutions continue to face challenges due to fragmented systems and manual administrative processes. Students often rely on multiple platforms or offline procedures for attendance, notices, classroom schedules, event registrations, grievance reporting, and accessing campus resources. This results in delays, miscommunication, reduced operational efficiency, and a fragmented campus experience.

With the growing adoption of digital technologies, there is an increasing need for a unified, intelligent, and scalable platform that connects students, faculty, and administrators while simplifying everyday campus operations.

Problem Statement

Design and develop an innovative Smart Campus Platform that transforms traditional college operations into a centralized digital ecosystem. The proposed solution should improve communication, streamline academic and administrative workflows, enhance student engagement, and provide seamless access to essential campus services through a web application, mobile application, or cross-platform solution. Participants are encouraged to leverage Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Data Analytics, Blockchain, or Automation wherever relevant.

Objectives

  • Digitize and automate campus operations
  • Improve communication among students, faculty, and administration
  • Reduce paperwork and manual intervention
  • Enhance transparency and accessibility
  • Improve operational efficiency
  • Create a secure, user-friendly, and scalable platform
  • Deliver measurable improvements to the campus experience

Modules

  • Smart Attendance Management
  • Digital Notice & Communication
  • Event & Activity Management
  • Lost & Found Management
  • Complaint & Maintenance Portal
  • Academic & Timetable Management
  • Smart Campus Navigation
  • Student Dashboard
  • Faculty Dashboard
  • AI Campus Assistant (Bonus)
  • Campus Safety & Emergency Response (Bonus)
  • Student Community & Collaboration

Innovation

AI-powered Chatbot, Predictive Attendance Analytics, Student Performance Insights, Smart Digital ID Cards, IoT-enabled Smart Classrooms, Digital Library Management, Cashless Campus Solutions, Hostel Management, Transport Tracking, Smart Parking, Visitor Management, Carbon-Neutral Campus Monitoring, Accessibility Features, Multilingual Support, Offline-first Architecture.

Deliverables

Working prototype, Source code, Presentation (PPT), System architecture, Database design, UI/UX, Documentation, Live demonstration.

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any frontend, backend, cloud platform, database, AI/ML framework, and authentication technology.

Constraints

  • Build within the 4-hour hackathon duration
  • Open-source libraries and public APIs are allowed
  • AI-assisted development is permitted if participants can explain their implementation
  • Solutions should be original, secure, scalable, and user-friendly
