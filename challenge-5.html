Track 05 AgriTech Open Track

Smart Agriculture

Building Intelligent Solutions for Sustainable Farming and Food Security

Farmers face challenges such as unpredictable weather, crop diseases, water scarcity, rising input costs, low market transparency, and limited access to expert guidance.

🚀 Register for this Track ← Other Challenges

Background

Farmers face challenges such as unpredictable weather, crop diseases, water scarcity, rising input costs, low market transparency, and limited access to expert guidance. Many farming decisions are still based on manual observation, leading to reduced productivity and financial losses. Modern technologies can help farmers make informed decisions and improve agricultural sustainability.

Problem Statement

Design and develop an innovative AgriTech platform that improves farm productivity, resource management, crop health, and market access through intelligent digital technologies. Participants are encouraged to Integrate Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Internet of Things (IoT), Satellite Data, GIS, Cloud Computing, Robotics, and Data Analytics wherever applicable.

Challenge Objectives

  • Improve crop productivity and sustainability
  • Enable data-driven farming decisions
  • Optimize irrigation and resource usage
  • Detect crop diseases at an early stage
  • Improve farmer access to markets and information
  • Build scalable, affordable, and user-friendly solutions

Innovation Opportunities

Computer Vision for disease detection, drone crop monitoring, IoT soil sensors, AI yield prediction, precision agriculture, multilingual voice assistants, offline access for rural areas, blockchain-based food traceability, carbon farming analytics, and sustainable farming recommendations.

Expected Deliverables

Working Prototype (Web/Mobile/Desktop)
Source Code Repository
Project Presentation (PPT)
System Architecture Diagram
Database Design
UI/UX Design
Live Demonstration

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any frontend, backend, AI/ML framework, GIS tools, IoT platforms, drones, or satellite APIs.

Constraints

  • Build within the 4-hour hackathon duration
  • Open-source tools and APIs are permitted
  • AI-assisted development is allowed if participants understand their implementation
  • Solutions should be practical, affordable, and suitable for real-world agricultural use
