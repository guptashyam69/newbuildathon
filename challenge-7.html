Background

Healthcare providers continue to face challenges such as fragmented patient records, inefficient appointment systems, medication non-adherence, delayed emergency response, and limited healthcare accessibility. Modern technologies can help create secure, intelligent, and scalable healthcare solutions that improve patient care and operational efficiency.

Problem Statement

Design and develop a Smart Healthcare Platform that improves healthcare accessibility, patient management, preventive care, and emergency response using technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud Computing, Telemedicine, Wearables, and Data Analytics.

Objectives

  • Improve access to healthcare services
  • Digitize healthcare workflows
  • Enhance communication between patients and providers
  • Promote preventive healthcare
  • Reduce administrative workload
  • Ensure secure handling of medical data
  • Build a scalable and user-friendly platform

Modules

  • Digital Health Records (EHR)
  • Appointment & Hospital Management
  • AI Symptom Checker
  • Medicine Reminder & Prescription Management
  • Telemedicine Platform
  • Emergency Healthcare System
  • Remote Patient Monitoring
  • Preventive Healthcare Dashboard
  • Mental Health & Wellness
  • AI Healthcare Assistant (Bonus)
  • Healthcare Analytics Dashboard (Bonus)
  • Smart Blood & Organ Donation Platform (Bonus)

Innovation

AI-assisted disease prediction, wearable integration, blockchain-secured medical records, AI-powered emergency triage, personalized healthcare recommendations, maternal and elderly care monitoring, multilingual support, and offline healthcare access.

Deliverables

Working Prototype · Source Code · PPT · System Architecture · Database Design · UI/UX · Documentation · Live Demonstration

Technical Freedom

Teams may use any frontend, backend, database, cloud platform, AI/ML framework, IoT platform, or healthcare API.

Constraints

  • Build the solution within the 4-hour hackathon duration
  • Open-source tools and APIs are allowed
  • AI-assisted development is permitted if explainable
  • The solution must not replace professional medical diagnosis
  • Protect patient privacy and securely manage healthcare data
